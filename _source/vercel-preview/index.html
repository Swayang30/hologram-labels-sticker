The problem

When Your Label Identifies the Product — But Doesn’t Protect It.

A conventional label may carry your brand and product information, but it can leave gaps in authentication, tamper detection, traceability and brand presentation.

  1. 01

    Counterfeits Can Look Too Similar

    A standard printed label can be copied, making it difficult to distinguish an original product from an imitation.

  2. 02

    No Easy Way to Verify Authenticity

    Without a distinctive security feature, customers and channel partners have little to check against when authenticity is in doubt.

  3. 03

    Tampering Can Go Unnoticed

    If a label can be removed and reused without leaving a visible sign, product tampering becomes harder to detect.

  4. 04

    Your Label Doesn’t Reflect Your Brand

    A basic label may identify the product, but it can miss the opportunity to add a premium, distinctive visual element to the packaging.

Security hologram origination

How a security hologram authenticates

A security hologram can combine multiple layers of authentication — from features visible to the naked eye to elements designed for deeper verification. Different security levels can be built into the same holographic label, depending on your application.

  1. 1

    Overt — Features Your Customer Can See

    Colour-shifting and dynamic effects visible under normal light allow customers to recognise and check the security feature without special equipment.

  2. 2

    Covert — Features that need a decoder

    Hidden elements revealed under specific light or through a decoding film provide an additional layer of verification for authorised staff and distributors.

  3. 3

    Forensic — features for deeper verification

    Micro- and nano-level security elements support detailed examination and authentication when deeper verification is required.

DOVID and OVD technologies create dynamic, angle-dependent visual effects that add distinctive aesthetics while making authentication easier to recognise.

Powered by ENGRAVION™

For advanced OVD origination, ENGRAVION™ enables highly refined optical effects, including 3D imagery, depth and motion — allowing security features to become an integral part of the design rather than an add-on.

Brand protection

Tamper-Evident Protection & Serialised Authentication

Designed to reveal tampering and give every label a unique identity. Tamper-evident constructions can show visible signs of attempted removal, while serialised data adds an additional layer of authentication and traceability.

  • Tamper-Evident Patterns VOID, checkerboard, honeycomb or customised release patterns designed to reveal attempted removal or reuse.
  • Serial numbering Unique sequential or variable numbers that help identify individual labels and support authentication.
  • Barcodes and QR codes Add machine-readable data for verification, identification and track-and-trace applications.
  • Reels and Sheets Available in reel or sheet formats for manual, semi-automatic or machine application.

Every label can be built with a combination of tamper evidence and variable data—helping protect the product while supporting authentication across the supply chain.
